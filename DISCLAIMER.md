## English

CiteProof is an informational tool for checking bibliographic references. Its automated results are based on records returned by third-party scholarly databases and matching rules. It does not independently establish that a work exists, assess its scientific quality, or certify the accuracy of a bibliography.

**Results are not guaranteed to be 100% accurate, complete, current, or suitable for a particular purpose.** Database coverage, incorrect or missing metadata, publication versions, approximate matching, rate limits, and service failures can cause false positives, false negatives, or inconclusive results. The CLI queries Crossref, DBLP, Semantic Scholar, OpenAlex and arXiv; the browser UI does not query arXiv directly.

“Verified” describes a database match, not a guarantee that every field is correct. “Likely hallucinated” is a signal for review, not proof of fabrication or academic misconduct. A failed query, an unindexed page, or a difference from an author's recommended citation does not by itself establish that a reference is false. Original-page and search links are provided for manual checking and are not automatically verified.

Review original publications, publisher or author pages, model cards, and recommended citations before relying on a result.
